### Light Pollution

At night, above the prideful city's glow, \\
The stars withdraw their fire, like minor players \\
Who, when the protag takes the stage alone, \\
Crouch in the shadows of the wings, ahush, \\
Expectant of some dire revelation \\
Or hidden sorrow, nursed in solitude.\\
